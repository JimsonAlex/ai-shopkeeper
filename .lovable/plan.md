

# Shop AI Copilot — Landing Page Plan

## Hero Section
- Bold headline: "Bank-Grade Accounting for Chaotic Retail"
- Subheadline emphasizing the core value: record sales via voice, photo, or text — the system handles the accounting automatically
- Primary CTA: "Get Started Free" / "Join the Waitlist"
- Secondary CTA: "See How It Works"
- Hero visual: mockup or illustration showing a shop owner sending a voice note and the system producing clean financial records

## Problem Section — "The Retail Reality"
- 3-4 pain point cards with icons highlighting:
  - Money disappearing into pockets and drawers
  - No idea if you're actually profitable
  - Too busy selling to do bookkeeping
  - Existing tools are either too simple or too complex

## Solution Section — "Meet Your AI Copilot"
- Visual walkthrough of the core flow:
  1. **Speak, snap, or type** — record a sale in seconds
  2. **AI handles the math** — automatic double-entry accounting behind the scenes
  3. **Know your profit** — real-time dashboard showing cash, margins, and inventory
- Emphasis on "No accounting knowledge required"

## Key Features Grid
- **Automatic Double-Entry Accounting** — every transaction balanced, every time
- **Voice & Photo Input** — say it or snap it, the AI figures it out
- **Smart Inventory Tracking** — FIFO cost tracking, low stock alerts
- **Credit Sale Management** — track who owes you, get reminders
- **Daily Reconciliation** — know exactly where every shilling went
- **AI Fraud Detection** — spot shortages and suspicious patterns early

## Persona Section — "Built For You"
- Two cards showcasing the target users:
  - **Shop Owner (Musa)**: "Too busy to do bookkeeping? Just send a voice note."
  - **Shop Clerk (Aisha)**: "Prove every sale was done right. The system protects you."

## How It Works — 3-Step Visual
1. **Onboard in minutes** — AI asks simple questions: how much cash, what's in stock, who owes you
2. **Run your day** — record sales, expenses, and purchases naturally
3. **See the truth** — real-time profit, cash flow, and inventory at a glance

## Social Proof / Trust Section
- Placeholder for testimonials or early adopter quotes
- Trust badges: "Bank-grade accuracy", "Your data stays yours", "Built for African retail"

## Pricing Preview (Optional)
- Simple tier cards or a "Free to start" message with a CTA

## Footer
- Navigation links, contact info, social media links
- "Powered by Shop AI Copilot" branding

## Design Direction
- Clean, modern, and professional — but warm and approachable
- Color palette: deep navy/dark blue primary with vibrant green or orange accent (energy, African retail warmth)
- Large readable typography, generous whitespace
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Subtle animations on scroll for section reveals
